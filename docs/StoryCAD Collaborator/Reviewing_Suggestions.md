# Reviewing Suggestions

This is the most important habit in Collaborator: **read the suggestions, then choose**. Collaborator proposes; you dispose.

## Property Updates

After a run, the center list shows **Property Updates**. Each row is one field on a story element, for example `Overview.Premise` or `Problem.ProtGoal`.

A short header counts how many updates are waiting and how many are ready versus need your OK.

<!-- image: Property Updates list with count header and several rows -->

## Ready vs already has your text

Collaborator treats fields in two everyday ways:

| Situation | What it means | What bulk Accept does |
|-----------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Ready** (new or refresh) | Empty field, or a field Collaborator already wrote earlier in this session | Accept All applies these |
| **Has your text** | You (or your past edits) already filled the field | Accept All **skips** these; you must Review Each if you want to replace your words |

That protects your writing when you re-run a workflow on a half-filled outline.

## Accept All

**Accept All** applies every **ready** update and leaves every **has your text** field alone.

Use it when you started from empty fields and want the first pass written in quickly. After Accept All, open StoryCAD and skim the forms.

## Review Each

**Review Each** walks one field at a time.

For each field you see:

- **Yours** — what is in the outline now  
- **Proposed** — what Collaborator suggests  

Then choose:

| Button | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Accept** | Write the proposal into this field only |
| **Skip** | Keep yours; drop this proposal |
| **Accept Free Remaining** | Apply all remaining *ready* fields in one step; still leave “has your text” fields for Accept or Skip |

<!-- image: Review Each card showing Yours and Proposed -->

**Accept Free Remaining** is *not* “accept everything empty only.” It also applies refreshes of fields Collaborator already wrote this session. It never bulk-overwrites fields marked as having your text.

## Try Again

**Try Again** discards the current pending set for a new run of the same workflow (see [Running a Workflow](Running_a_Workflow.html)). Use it instead of accepting a weak pass.

## Nothing is permanent until you accept

Closing Collaborator without accepting leaves those fields as they were. Saving after accept keeps approved text with the outline.

If you accept a suggestion and dislike it later, edit the field in StoryCAD. You are not locked in.
